# Stallviken Design Specification
*Swedish Stable with Icelandic Horses - Brand & Color Guidelines*

---

## Brand Identity

### Concept
A harmonious blend of Swedish natural landscapes and the noble character of Icelandic horses. The design emphasizes trust, tradition, and connection to nature while maintaining a professional, approachable aesthetic.

### Visual Philosophy
- **Natural**: Rooted in Swedish countryside and forest aesthetics
- **Noble**: Reflecting the proud heritage of Icelandic horses
- **Trustworthy**: Professional yet warm and welcoming
- **Authentic**: Genuine connection to equestrian culture

## Color Palette

### Primary Colors

| Color | Hex Code | RGB | Usage |
|-------|----------|-----|-------|
| **Deep Forest Green** | `#2D4A3A` | `45, 74, 58` | Main brand color, headers, primary CTAs |
| **Warm Cream** | `#F5F1E8` | `245, 241, 232` | Primary background, card backgrounds |
| **Slate Blue-Grey** | `#5C6B73` | `92, 107, 115` | Body text, secondary elements |

### Accent Colors

| Color | Hex Code | RGB | Usage |
|-------|----------|-----|-------|
| **Icelandic Blue** | `#4A90A4` | `74, 144, 164` | Highlights, interactive elements, hover states |
| **Soft Birch White** | `#FEFEFE` | `254, 254, 254` | Text on dark backgrounds, clean contrast |
| **Sage Green** | `#87A96B` | `135, 169, 107` | Tertiary accents, borders, decorative elements |

### Color Relationships
- **Primary Contrast**: Deep Forest Green + Soft Birch White
- **Warm Pairing**: Warm Cream + Icelandic Blue
- **Natural Harmony**: Forest greens and blues create serene balance
- **Green Family**: Deep Forest Green + Sage Green for layered depth
- **Text Combinations**: Slate Blue-Grey on Warm Cream (optimal readability)

---

## Typography Guidelines

### Hierarchy
- **H1**: Deep Forest Green, bold weight
- **H2-H3**: Slate Blue-Grey, medium weight
- **Body Text**: Slate Blue-Grey on Warm Cream
- **Links**: Icelandic Blue, underline on hover
- **Captions**: Sage Green, smaller size

### Accessibility
- Minimum contrast ratio: 4.5:1 for normal text
- 3:1 for large text (18pt+ or 14pt+ bold)
- All color combinations tested for WCAG AA compliance

## UI Component Colors

### Navigation
- **Background**: Deep Forest Green
- **Text**: Soft Birch White
- **Hover**: Icelandic Blue
- **Active**: Sage Green

### Buttons

#### Primary Button
- **Background**: Deep Forest Green
- **Text**: Soft Birch White
- **Hover**: Darken by 10%
- **Border**: None

#### Secondary Button
- **Background**: Icelandic Blue
- **Text**: Soft Birch White
- **Hover**: Deeper blue (#3A7A8A)
- **Border**: None

#### Tertiary Button
- **Background**: Transparent
- **Text**: Slate Blue-Grey
- **Hover**: Warm Cream background
- **Border**: 1px Slate Blue-Grey

### Cards & Containers
- **Background**: Soft Birch White
- **Border**: 1px Warm Cream or Sage Green (subtle)
- **Shadow**: Soft grey (#00000010)
- **Header**: Deep Forest Green accent

### Forms
- **Input Background**: Soft Birch White
- **Input Border**: Slate Blue-Grey (light)
- **Focus Border**: Icelandic Blue
- **Label Text**: Slate Blue-Grey
- **Placeholder**: Sage Green (light)

## Technical Specifications

### Web Implementation
```css
:root {
  --primary-green: #2D4A3A;
  --warm-cream: #F5F1E8;
  --slate-grey: #5C6B73;
  --icelandic-blue: #4A90A4;
  --birch-white: #FEFEFE;
  --sage-green: #87A96B;
}
```

### Print Colors (CMYK)
- Deep Forest Green: C70 M30 Y60 K20
- Warm Cream: C3 M3 Y8 K0
- Slate Blue-Grey: C25 M15 Y10 K15
- Icelandic Blue: C55 M12 Y25 K5
- Sage Green: C30 M0 Y40 K10

### Accessibility Notes
- All text color combinations meet WCAG AA standards
- Color is never the only means of conveying information
- Alternative text provided for all color-coded elements
- High contrast mode compatible
